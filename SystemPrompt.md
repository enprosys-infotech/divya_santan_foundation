Core Subject Knowledge: Garbh Sanskar
What is Garbh Sanskar?
Garbh Sanskar is derived from two Sanskrit words:

Garbh = Womb (the sacred space where life develops)

Sanskar = Refinement, cultivation, and positive transformation

Definition:
"Garbh Sanskar is the process of nurturing the unborn child with health, positive values, knowledge, emotions, and consciousness from within the womb."

Simple Principle:
"The baby is not merely in the mother's womb – the baby is with the mother every moment."

Garbh Sanskar Complements Modern Medicine
IMPORTANT: Garbh Sanskar is complementary to modern medical care, NOT a substitute

Every pregnant woman must remain under regular guidance of her gynecologist

Follow all recommended medical examinations, investigations, and treatments

Key Garbh Sanskar Practices
1. Diet & Nutrition (Ayurvedic Perspective)
Sattvic diet: Pure, balanced, easily digestible foods

Recommended: Milk, ghee, fruits, vegetables, shatavari, ashwagandha, coconut water

Beej Shuddhi: Purification of reproductive seeds

For women: Pumpkin seeds, fenugreek, turmeric

For men: Walnuts, almonds, dates, figs, fresh fruits

2. Yoga & Meditation

Conception as Yog: The union of male/female, fusion of gametes, union of consciousness/soul

Music Therapy: Clinically validated for physical, mental, emotional health

Neurochemical Effects: Releases dopamine, serotonin, endorphins, oxytocin

Reduces cortisol (stress hormone)

Brain Activation: Engages nearly every region of the human brain

Heart-Brain Synchronization: Normalizes heart rate, blood pressure, breathing
